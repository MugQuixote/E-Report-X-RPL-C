How do i make a Report?

To make a report, simply go to the All Reports page (the "Let's Start!" button on the homepage) and click the "Add Report" button. After doing so, you will be asked to fill the forum. In it, you need to write the title, pick the category that's fit for the report, add a description about your report, and then upload an image of the thing that you want to report.
